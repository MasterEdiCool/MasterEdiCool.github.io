Project

Mousey the Junkbot

A light-seeking robot built from salvaged computer parts, sensors, and LEDs — a hands-on study in sensor-driven autonomy and electronics reuse.

Sept 2024 – June 2025

Notebook

Overview

Mousey the Junkbot is a small autonomous robot built almost entirely from salvaged computer parts — repurposed motors, sensors, structural plastics, and LEDs — designed to seek out light sources and respond to its environment with mouselike behavior.

The project explores how much real autonomy can be built from e-waste, and how tightly hardware constraints can be coupled with simple, legible control code.
