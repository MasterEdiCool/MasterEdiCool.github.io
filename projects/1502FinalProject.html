Project

Circuit Blocks

A hands-on circuit-building puzzle game for 5th graders where players arrange electronic component blocks on a grid to complete a working circuit.

Jan 2026 – Apr 2026

Notebook

Overview

Circuit Blocks is a hands-on puzzle game where players arrange and orient 3D-printed component boxes using common electronic components like LEDs, buzzers, a motor, a button, and a potentiometer and wire boxes on a grid board to complete a working electrical circuit, with tactile feedback like a light turning on, a buzzer sounding, or a motor spinning to signal success.

Designed for 5th graders, the game teaches foundational electronics concepts — including how simple circuits work, polarity, and parallel circuits — through engaging, problem-solving gameplay that builds STEM curiosity in an age-appropriate, bilingual (English/Spanish) format.
